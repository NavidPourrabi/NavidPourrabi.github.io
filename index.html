About

I came to product through film, and kept the one habit that matters: watch people before deciding what they need.

I studied cinema editing at Soore University in Tehran, then spent years cutting films, making motion graphics, and teaching high-school students to make their own short films. Editing is mostly deciding what to leave out. So is product work.

On Extendsell I spend as much time in a spreadsheet as in Figma. I built our list of 110 Shopify stores and matched each one to the feature that fits it, set discovery targets we could actually hit alongside a master's, worked through Shopify App Store review, and ran my share of the Product Hunt launch outreach. Then I design what we decided to build. Doing both means the decision and the screen never drift apart.

Teaching turned out to be good practice too. Getting a room of teenagers to commit to a plan and finish two films a year is not so different from getting a team to agree on a roadmap.

I moved to the Netherlands for my master's in Digital Media Studies at Tilburg University, and I'm staying — I'm teaching myself Dutch. At university I study how interfaces shape the people using them. Outside it I'm watching films, or deep in Star Wars, The Witcher, mythology, or history. Anything with a good story.

How I work

Four steps, and the third one is the job.

01

Talk to the people it's for

Merchants, shoppers, reviews, support tickets. Find where things actually break before deciding anything.

02

Name the tension

Most products have two users pulling opposite ways. Say it out loud and the scope decisions get much easier.

03

Decide what not to build

Set a target small enough to hit, cut what doesn't move it, then prototype what's left.

04

Ship, then watch

Nothing is finished until someone who isn't me has used it. Then back to step one.
