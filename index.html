About me

I studied cinema in Tehran, at Soore University, and then worked for years as a video editor and motion designer. I also taught short filmmaking at a high school. My students made at least two films a year, and three of them won best student film at local festivals.

Editing is mostly deciding what to cut. That turned out to be most of product work too. The other thing film gave me is caring about the person on the other side of the screen, and whether they actually get what you meant.

In 2025 I moved to the Netherlands for my master's at Tilburg. At university I study how apps and platforms shape the people who use them. At work I try to make things that are honest with people. I'm staying here, and I'm teaching myself Dutch. Langzaam maar zeker.

I learn best by doing. I'd rather build a small version, show it to someone, and fix it than plan for weeks.
