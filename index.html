About

I don't hand off a screen and walk away. I stay until the thing works in the market.

I work as a UI/UX designer and a product manager, and I think the split between those two jobs is mostly artificial. The research that tells you what to build is the same research that tells you how it should feel. So I do both: I talk to users, frame the problem, design the interface, write the requirements, and sit with engineering through the build.

Most of my recent work is in e-commerce — merchant-facing tools where every design decision has a number attached to it. On Extendsell I designed the add-ons and frequently-bought-together experience, which meant balancing two very different users: the merchant configuring offers in the admin, and the shopper who should never feel sold to.

My rule of thumb: if I can't explain why a screen exists in one sentence, it shouldn't ship yet.

How I work
